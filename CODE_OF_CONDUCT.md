# Code of Conduct

VelarScript Libraries is a technical community built around respectful,
evidence-based collaboration.

Contributors must be welcoming, assume good faith, discuss the work rather than
the person, and respect privacy and consent. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publication of
private information are not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the
maintainers listed on the GitHub organization rather than in a public issue.
Reports will be reviewed confidentially and without retaliation.

This policy applies to repository activity and to public spaces where someone
is representing the project.
